Our Story

Craftsmanship Rooted in
Three Decades of Trust

Konark Overseas is a Chennai-based manufacturer and exporter of premium leather garments and finished leather, with a heritage spanning over three decades. Our commitment to superior quality, ethical production, and consistent craftsmanship has earned us enduring partnerships with fashion houses across the Globe.

Every skin that passes through our facility is a testament to our founding principle: that excellence is not achieved once — it is practiced daily.

1990
Founded as Navlac Leathers — supplying finished leather to garment manufacturers in Italy and Germany.
Early 2000s
Expanded into leather garment manufacturing under Shrinkar Garments, serving Italy, Germany, USA, Switzerland, and Sweden.
Present
Operating as Konark Overseas — delivering both finished leather and premium leather garments to clients across Europe.
